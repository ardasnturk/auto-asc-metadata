Your store listing, built from your code.

Vitrin reads your app project — Swift, Flutter, React Native, Expo or Android — studies the competition and produces ASO-ready metadata, screenshots and App Previews for the App Store and Google Play. You choose what to make, approve every item, and only then does it upload.

Claude decides. You choose. The CLI does.

Creative work — copy, keywords, art direction, shot list, video plan — is written by Claude into schema-checked JSON. The choices that are yours are asked, never assumed. Everything deterministic is a TypeScript CLI you can read and test.

  1. 1

    Analyze

    Detects the project kind and every platform the app ships on, with evidence. Reads .pbxproj, Info.plist, String Catalogs, pubspec.yaml, app.json, Gradle files and code signals into an app profile where every feature points to a file.

  2. 2

    Research

    Searches the App Store per storefront, reads competitors' screenshots with on-device OCR, extracts palettes and scores keyword candidates by relevance, popularity and competition.

  3. 3

    Create

    Builds and captures your app per platform — simulators, the Mac window or an Android emulator — then renders screenshots, creatives and App Previews in the design you picked.

  4. 4

    Review & upload

    Validates against the stores' rules, shows everything in a review gallery, and uploads exactly what you approved with fastlane and the store APIs. Then it reads back what the store saved.

You choose. Every run.

Vitrin asks before it makes anything, and every stage, the review and the upload follow your answers. Nothing outside your selection is touched in the store.

You see it all before the store does

vitrin review --serve opens a local gallery of everything that would be uploaded — every screenshot per store, platform, locale and target in upload order, App Previews, creatives, event cards and all metadata with counters, with validation issues inline.

  • Approve everything, or reject single items with a comment — Claude fixes them at the source and shows you again.
  • The approval records a hash of every file. Any later change, including Claude's own fixes, needs a new approval.
  • A real upload refuses unapproved or changed content — --yes included.
  • The dry-run plan lists what will be replaced and what stays untouched.

Truthful by design. Caught before Apple does.

Every headline, badge, video beat and claim must link to a feature with evidence in your code (App Review 2.3). Ratings come from the live App Store; testimonials only from your own reviews, verbatim. One command checks the rest and tells Claude exactly what to fix.

  • Placeholder text, Apple-designated recognitions and Kids Category wording in metadata
  • A generic What's New when the release adds features
  • Prices, website addresses, copyright symbols or placeholders on screenshots
  • Keyword field over 100 bytes, words repeated from the name, competitor brands
  • Google Play title and description policy: promo words, emoji, URLs
  • Alpha channels, wrong pixel sizes, 60 fps or silent App Previews

Everything a listing needs

Metadata for both stores

App Store name, subtitle, keyword field, description, promo text and What's New; Google Play title, short and full description and changelog. Grapheme and UTF-8 byte counting — alışkanlık is 10 characters but 13 bytes.

Screenshot engine

HTML templates rendered with Playwright: 12 presets, realistic CSS device frames for every family, feature-wall and mosaic closing shots, Play feature graphic and icon, RTL, exact pixel sizes without alpha.

Screens from your code

No build needed: Claude reads your views and recreates each screen in HTML with realistic demo data and your app's own texts; Vitrin renders it per language and device with a 9:41 status bar. Prefer real captures? It also builds with xcodebuild, flutter build, CocoaPods, expo prebuild or Gradle, and captures in dedicated simulators (9:41, per-locale language), the Mac window, or an Android emulator in demo mode.

Safe, partial uploads

You pick the App Store uploader: the asc CLI (no Ruby) or fastlane deliver, one run per Apple platform; supply for Google Play (listing and images only). Partial selections never delete or overwrite anything you did not select. Read-back after every upload.

Tidy repository

A managed .gitignore block, kept up to date by every command, so generated files are never committed. Keys stay in .env and are masked in every log.

Idempotent and resumable

Stages track their input hashes and skip what hasn't changed. Missing keys don't stop generation — the upload simply waits until vitrin doctor passes.

Install in one line

Vitrin is a Claude Code plugin. Requires macOS, Node.js 22+ and Claude Code; Xcode, Ruby + Bundler and ffmpeg for Apple platforms, the Flutter SDK or Android SDK when your app needs them. vitrin doctor checks them and tells you what is missing.

  1. Install the plugin

    claude plugin install vitrin --marketplace ardasnturk/vitrin

    or inside Claude Code:

    /plugin marketplace add ardasnturk/vitrin
/plugin install vitrin@vitrin

  2. Open your app in Claude Code

    cd path/to/YourApp
claude

    No clone of Vitrin needed. It first asks which language to talk in (English by default). On a new repo it creates vitrin.config.yaml, .env.example and the .gitignore block; fastlane files only if you choose fastlane. Keys are asked for at the upload and go in .env — never in the chat.

  3. Type /vitrin

    /vitrin

    The first run installs the CLI for the plugin's version — one time, about 450 MB including Chromium, no sudo. Then Claude asks for platforms, content and design, works through every stage and waits for your “yes”.

FAQ

Does it work with Flutter, React Native or Android apps?

Yes. Swift/SwiftUI apps are the primary case; Flutter, React Native, Expo and native Android projects are detected automatically, built with their own tools and captured in simulators or an Android emulator. Vitrin produces App Store content for iPhone, iPad, iPhone Duo, Mac, Apple Watch, Apple TV and Vision Pro, and Google Play listings with phone and tablet screenshots, the feature graphic and the icon. It runs on macOS.

How do I install it? Do I have to clone the repo?

No cloning. Run claude plugin install vitrin --marketplace ardasnturk/vitrin (or in Claude Code: /plugin marketplace add ardasnturk/vitrin, then /plugin install vitrin@vitrin). The first time /vitrin runs, the CLI installs itself into ~/.local/share/vitrin/<version>/ — about 450 MB including Chromium, once per version. Update with claude plugin update vitrin@vitrin.

Do I need an OpenAI or Anthropic API key?

No. The creative work happens inside your Claude Code session through the /vitrin skill. The CLI itself makes no LLM calls.

What exactly gets uploaded?

Only the platforms and content you selected, and only after you approved them in the review gallery. Selected screenshot sets are replaced per display type; unselected sets in the store are never deleted or overwritten. On Google Play, Vitrin uploads listing text and images only — never binaries, tracks or rollouts.

Will it upload anything without asking?

No. Every image, video and text is shown to you first. The upload starts with a dry-run plan, needs your explicit “yes”, and refuses content that differs from what you approved. Submitting for review needs vitrin submit --confirm <bundleId>. Prices, releases and the age-rating and privacy questionnaires stay with you.

What happens to the generated files?

They are never committed: Vitrin keeps a managed block in .gitignore. After a checked upload you decide whether to keep the uploaded set in metadata/ or delete it. Research downloads (HTTP cache, competitor screenshots, OCR files) are removed either way.

Does it need to build my app?

No. By default Claude recreates your screens in HTML from the source code, so there is no build, no simulator and no demo mode. If you want real captures instead (vitrin select --capture launch-args), it builds your app for each selected platform and opens each screen with launch arguments in dedicated simulators with a 9:41 status bar and the right system language, in the Mac window, or in an Android emulator in System UI demo mode. The docs show how to add a demo-data mode in Swift, Flutter, React Native/Expo and Kotlin.

Can it create the app record in App Store Connect?

Apple doesn't allow that with an API key. Create the app once in App Store Connect; Vitrin stops and tells you if it's missing.

What about licenses?

Vitrin is MIT licensed. It bundles OFL fonts and Lucide icons; the style presets are adapted from ParthJadhav/app-store-screenshots (MIT). App Previews are rendered with Remotion, which is free for individuals and companies with up to three employees; larger teams need a Remotion company license.

Is this an Apple or Google product?

No. Vitrin is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Apple Inc. or Google LLC.
