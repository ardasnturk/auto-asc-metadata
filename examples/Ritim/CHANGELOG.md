# Changelog

## 1.2.0 (12)

### Added
- Statistics tab with a weekly completion chart, a 30-day trend and current streaks per habit.
- 12-week calendar heatmap on the habit detail screen.
- CSV export of all check-ins and sharing a progress summary.
- Search in the habit list.
- Ritim Plus monthly subscription with a one-week free trial.

### Improved
- iPad layout with an adaptive sidebar and multi-column grids.
- Larger text support and VoiceOver labels for check-in rings, icon and color pickers.
- Turkish translation reviewed for every screen.

### Fixed
- Streaks no longer reset in the morning before you have checked in for the day.
