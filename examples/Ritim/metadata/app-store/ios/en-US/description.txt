Build habits that stick with one tap a day.
Keep your streaks alive and watch your progress grow in clear charts.
Simple, calm and focused on what you do every day.

Ritim helps you turn small daily actions into a steady rhythm. Add the habits that matter to you, set a daily goal and check them in with a single tap on the progress ring.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Check in habits with one tap and see today's progress at a glance
• Keep your streaks alive with current and best streak counts
• See your progress in clear charts: weekly completion and a 30-day trend
• Review 12 weeks of history in a calendar heatmap for each habit
• Get a gentle daily reminder for each habit
• Search your habit list, share your progress and export check-ins as CSV
• Personalise every habit with an icon, a colour and a daily goal

MADE FOR IPHONE AND IPAD
On iPad, Ritim uses an adaptive sidebar and multi-column layouts. Light and dark mode, larger text and VoiceOver labels are supported.

RITIM PLUS
Ritim Plus is an optional monthly auto-renewing subscription with a one-week free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

Available in English and Turkish.
