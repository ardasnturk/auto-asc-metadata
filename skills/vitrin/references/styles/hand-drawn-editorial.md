# Hand-Drawn Editorial (`hand-drawn-editorial`)

> Condensed from the "05 Hand-Drawn Editorial Tasks" spec in [app-store-screenshots](https://github.com/ParthJadhav/app-store-screenshots) by Parth Jadhav (MIT) — see [README](README.md#credits-and-licence).

Superlist: solid navy / purple / cream / coral slides, a clean sans headline with one brush-script word in coral and a squiggle, tilted phones, doodles, a lowercase wordmark.

## Typography

Inter 600, ~0.118 × width; the script word in Caveat 700 at 1.5×, rotated −3°, with a squiggle underline. Wordmark: app name lowercase, top-left.

## Headline emphasis

Exactly one script phrase per slide.

## Background and decoration

Solid color + 3 % grain (+ soft vignette on dark). Doodles in the accent: asterisk, squiggle, outline star near the device; the feature wall alternates sans and script lines. Density: editorial (≥ 2 doodles).

## Device and framing

Black frame, warm soft shadow, tilted 12° alternating. Rhythm: tilted → tilted → tilted → two-devices → tilted (the planner keeps adjacent framings different).

## Copy tone

Designer-friendly: "The one app that fits *your whole day*", "Tasks and notes *together.*"

## Avoid

Two script words, thin monoline scripts, gradients.
