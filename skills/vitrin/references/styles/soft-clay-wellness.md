## Typography

Fraunces (SOFT 100, opsz 144) 420, ~0.128 × width, tracking −0.025em. Labels DM Sans uppercase 0.2em.

## Headline emphasis

One italic word in clay ink.

## Background and decoration

Warm radial base, 5 % grain; clay objects with three-stop fill, inner light/shadow, specular and warm cast shadow; hills overlap the device bottom 6–12 %. Density: editorial (2–5 objects).

## Device and framing

Silver frame, warm shadow, upright (one slide may tilt ≤ 3°). Arch window behind the hero. Rhythm: bleed-bottom → bleed-top → offset → bleed-bottom → two-devices. Use `inverted` once for an evening/sleep feature.

## Copy tone

Slow and sensory: "Taste each bag at its *peak*.", "Sip, then *sleep* easy."

## Avoid

Saturated hues, plastic-looking blobs, more than five props, grey shadows.
