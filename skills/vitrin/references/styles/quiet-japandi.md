## Palette (as rendered)

| Token | Value |
|---|---|
| Washi | `#FCFAF6` → `#F3F0EA` → `#E2DBCF`, sumi `#1E1D1B` |
| Labels | stone ink `#4E4B46` |
| Kenten dots | hinoki ink `#7A5A34` (mixed with the brand) |
| Seal | shu `#C8321E`, exactly once per slide — never brand-tinted |

## Typography

Shippori Mincho 500, ~0.118 × width, leading 1.08, flush left in a column ≤ 68 % wide. Labels DM Sans uppercase 0.32em; the kicker runs vertically beside the seal.

## Headline emphasis

Kenten: one small dot under each letter of the key word.

## Background and decoration

Washi wash + seeded fibre strands + 3 % grain. The hanko shows the app's initial. Density: minimal (seal + one object).

## Device and framing

Silver frame, upright, standing on the shelf with a warm contact shadow (no bleed). Rhythm: offset (alternating sides) → bleed-bottom … ; `inverted` gives a sumi slide.

## Copy tone

Quiet, few words: "Beans at their *best*.", "Only what you *need*."

## Avoid

Centred or bold headlines, color emphasis, a floating phone, a second red mark.
