## Typography

Inter Tight 600, ~0.116 × width, tracking −0.035em, leading 1.02. Eyebrow (kicker) JetBrains Mono uppercase, tracking 0.15em, with a lavender dot.

## Headline emphasis

Whole headline gradient-lit; one phrase lit by the beam.

## Background and decoration

Conic beam dimmed to ≤ 30 % under the text band, dot grid masked out of the text, horizon line, vignette, 5 % grain. Badges are dark glass with a lavender edge; a comet line points at the device. Density: editorial.

## Device and framing

Titanium frame with lavender rim light, black contact shadow, upright. Rhythm: bleed-bottom → offset → bleed-bottom → two-devices → offset.

## Copy tone

A senior engineer's changelog: "Brew at *peak.* Every bag.", "Ship on *Friday.* Sleep anyway." Short, exact, periods.

## Avoid

A second hue, light glass, glow on all four sides of the phone.
