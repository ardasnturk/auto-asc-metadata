# Magazine Cover Editorial (`magazine-cover-editorial`)

> Condensed from the "10 Magazine Cover Editorial" spec in [app-store-screenshots](https://github.com/ParthJadhav/app-store-screenshots) by Parth Jadhav (MIT) — see [README](README.md#credits-and-licence).

Kinfolk: uncoated paper, a masthead, a roman cover line plus a larger swash-italic phrase in one spot color, and a color plate behind the phone like a cover photograph.

## Palette (as rendered)

| Token | Value |
|---|---|
| Paper | `#F1EBE1` (inverted: `#E7DECF`), ink `#1A1714` |
| Spot color | oxblood `#7A2320` — with a brand, the brand (darkened to ≥ 4.5:1) becomes the single spot color for the italic, plate and seal |

## Typography

Fraunces 400 (opsz 144, WONK italic), ~0.138 × width roman, italic phrase 1.2× larger, leading 0.94, left aligned. Small caps masthead in Fraunces.

## Headline emphasis

One swash-italic phrase in the spot color, larger than the roman line.

## Background and decoration

Paper tooth radials + 5 % grain; masthead (double rule, `APP · Nº 01 · SECTION · 1 / 6`); the spot-color plate behind the lower phone (hanging from the top when the caption is at the bottom); a letterpress seal with the app name. Density: editorial.

## Device and framing

Silver frame, oxblood-tinted shadow, upright. Rhythm: offset → bleed-bottom → offset → two-devices.

## Copy tone

Cover lines: "The ritual, *refined.*", "Read a little *slower.*"

## Avoid

Bold display serif, two spot colors, polaroid frames.
