# Noir Gold Luxe (`noir-gold-luxe`)

> Vitrin's own preset, distilled from the black-and-gold premium sets in the store galleries (recovery, reading, finance and membership apps).

Black lacquer and gilt: a warm glow and fine guilloche rings where the device stands, a double gilt rule inset like a certificate's border, a serif headline with a gold gradient on the one emphasised phrase and the app name centred on top between two short rules.

## Typography

Playfair Display 600, ~0.122 × width, leading 1.04, centred. Labels Inter uppercase with 0.28em tracking.

## Headline emphasis

A gold gradient across the phrase.

## Background and decoration

Radial lacquer lit at the device, a gold glow sized from the canvas's short side, guilloche rings masked to fade out before the caption, a double gilt rule inset from the edge and a centred app-name label. Slides without a device get a gilt sunburst rising from the bottom. Badges are dark glass with a gold rim.

## Device and framing

Titanium frame with a gold rim light, glow shadow, upright. Rhythm: bleed-bottom → offset → bleed-bottom → two-devices → offset.

## Copy tone

Calm and assured, few words: "Reclaim your mornings", "Your library, curated". Serif type carries the weight; no exclamation marks.

## Avoid

Saturated second colors, playful stickers, heavy sans badges. One inverted cream slide at most.
