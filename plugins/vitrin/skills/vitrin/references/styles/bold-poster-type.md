# Bold Poster Type (`bold-poster-type`)

> Vitrin's own preset, distilled from the condensed-type sets in the store galleries (habit, study and utility apps that read like gig posters).

A printed poster: ink, paper and one signal color in turn, giant condensed uppercase type with the emphasis word stuck on a solid box, a trim line inset from the edge and a halftone patch near the device.

| Ink slide | `#121212`, paper type `#F4EFE6`, emphasis box signal `#C6F432` with ink type |
| Paper slide | `#F4EFE6`, ink type, emphasis box ink with paper type |
| Signal slide | `#C6F432`, ink type, emphasis box ink with signal type |

Surfaces cycle ink → paper → signal. The brand replaces the signal everywhere (backgrounds included) only when it is as bright: at least 7:1 against the ink.

## Typography

Anton uppercase, ~0.168 × width, leading 0.98, flush left, up to three lines. Labels Barlow Condensed uppercase, body Inter Tight.

## Headline emphasis

`box`: an upright solid block behind the phrase, sized from the leading so it never covers the lines around it. Keep the boxed phrase to one or two short words; it does not wrap.

## Background and decoration

Flat stock, a hairline trim inset from the edge, a halftone patch in the device's corner, an index row (`APP ——— Nº 01`) inside the trim. A statement slide carries its number in outline in the lower corner; the cross-canvas moment is hazard tape in ink and signal. Badges are mono labels.

## Device and framing

Black frame, crisp shadow, upright, hard crop on offset slides. Rhythm: bleed-bottom → offset → bleed-top → bleed-bottom → two-devices. A slide whose device bleeds off the top skips the index row.

## Copy tone

Short imperatives and three-beat lines: "Tap. Check. Repeat.", "Don't break the chain.", "The numbers don't lie." Three to five words.

## Avoid

Long headlines (condensed type gets tall fast), a second accent color, rounded chips, gradients. Feature walls work well here: every other line gets the box.
