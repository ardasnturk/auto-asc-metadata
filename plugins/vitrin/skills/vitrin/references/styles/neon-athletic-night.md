## Typography

Anton (skewed −9°), ~0.19 × width, uppercase, leading 0.9, left aligned; labels Barlow Condensed 700 italic. Eyebrow: `APP / KICKER`.

## Headline emphasis

One volt word or number (color only).

## Background and decoration

Gradient + floodlight that pans per slide + carbon hairlines + vignette + 8 % grain; volt slash band behind the phone, speed streaks with chromatic fringe trailing off it. Density: editorial.

## Device and framing

Black frame, volt rim light, deep black shadow, tilted 8° alternating. Rhythm: tilted → tilted → two-devices → tilted → offset.

## Copy tone

Imperative, uppercase, two or three lines: "KNOW YOUR *NUMBER.*", "RUN THE *SPLIT.*"

## Avoid

A third hue, volt on light backgrounds, upright phones, icons or trophies.
