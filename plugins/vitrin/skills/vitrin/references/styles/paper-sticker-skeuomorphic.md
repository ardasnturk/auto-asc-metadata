# Paper Sticker Skeuomorphic (`paper-sticker-skeuomorphic`)

> Condensed from the "03 Paper Sticker Skeuomorphic" spec in [app-store-screenshots](https://github.com/ParthJadhav/app-store-screenshots) by Parth Jadhav (MIT) — see [README](README.md#credits-and-licence).

A student's binder: construction-paper slabs (peach, mint, putty, rose), marker-blue handwritten headlines with a highlighter band and squiggle, washi tape on the phone, stars and polka dots.

## Typography

Caveat 700 (marker substitute), ~0.168 × width, rotated −2°, left aligned; body DM Sans 600. The kicker becomes a sticky note.

## Headline emphasis

Highlighter band and a red squiggle under the phrase (the whole headline is the marker).

## Background and decoration

Paper slab + fibre lines + multiply grain + soft vignette; stars, polka-dot clusters, a hand-drawn arrow in the gap beside the device; washi tape across the phone corner; badges as paper labels on the device. Density: maximal.

## Device and framing

Graphite frame (the real bezel — no paper cut-out frames), soft shadow, ±3° on tilted slides. Rhythm: bleed-bottom → tilted → offset → two-devices → tilted.

## Copy tone

Friendly, a little scrappy: "Keep your *streak* alive", "Never *lose* a note".

## Avoid

Pure white paper, thin marker strokes, stickers over the headline.
